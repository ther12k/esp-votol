📐 Design System Specifications for Designer Agent

  • Target Device: Android Smartphone (390×844 to 412×915 viewport), Material 3 Dark theme.
  • Background: #07090D (Deep obsidian black).
  • Card Surfaces: #161B27 (Control Hero card), #11151F (Config/Pairing cards), with 1dp border #2A3242.
  • Typography: Clean geometric sans-serif (Inter / Roboto) + Monospace (JetBrains Mono) for status replies and keys.
  • Accent Colors: Amber #F59E0B (Brand & Badges), Emerald #10B981 (Disarmed & Safe), Crimson #EF4444 (Armed & Panic), Cyan #00C8FF (Battery Voltage).
  • Primary CTA: 76dp height, corner radius 20dp, context-aware toggle button on the Control tab.
  • Navigation: Material 3 Bottom Navigation Bar (72dp height, background #0B0E14, active pill indicator).
